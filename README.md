## 📋 Executive Summary

Dashboards provide real-time visibility, but static visualizations often hide complex operational dynamics. This repository houses an advanced **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** workflow built in **Python** (executed within **Deepnote's** collaborative cloud environment) analyzing high-frequency industrial sensor telemetry from an iron ore flotation plant. 

Rather than jumping straight into predictive modeling, this project establishes ground-truth engineering baseline metrics, uncovers variable interdependencies across chemical reagent feeds, and diagnoses shift-level anomalies over **737,000+ process records**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives & Core Questions

In mineral processing (specifically iron ore flotation), the primary objective is dual-fold:
1. **Maximize Yield:** Increase `% Iron Concentrate`.
2. **Minimize Impurity:** Reduce `% Silica Concentrate`.

Achieving optimal efficiency requires precise balance across continuous process parameters including chemical reagent flow rates (Starch, Amina), slurry pH levels, pulp flow, and flotation tank fluid levels. This analysis addresses three critical operational questions:
* **Baseline Health:** What are the operational ranges, variances, and distributional spreads across our primary sensor feeds?
* **Variable Interdependence:** Do primary levers (such as Ore Pulp pH and Starch/Amina reagent flow) directly drive target iron recovery and impurity reduction?
* **Anomaly Identification:** How do critical parameters behave over a 24-hour operating window (e.g., June 1, 2017 shift), and where do process instabilities occur?

## 📊 Analytical Workflow & Key Findings

### Step 1: Baseline Summary Statistics (`df.describe()`)
Evaluating over **737,000 process records** established standard operating baselines versus extreme plant outliers:
* **Feed Metrics:** `% Iron Feed` averaged **56.29%** (ranging from 42.74% to 65.78%), while `% Silica Feed` averaged **14.65%** (ranging from 1.31% to 33.4%).
* **Reagent Flow Dynamics:** `Starch Flow` exhibited substantial operational variance, averaging **2,869.14** with a standard deviation of **1,215.20** and extreme peaks reaching **6,300.23**.
* **Slurry Volume:** `Ore Pulp Flow` remained highly stable with a mean of **397.58** and a narrow standard deviation of **9.70**, indicating consistent throughput volume into the flotation circuits.

### Step 2: Shift-Level Deep-Dive (Datetime Indexing)
Using Python datetime indexing to isolate a 24-hour operating window (**June 1, 2017**), we tracked fine-grained temporal interactions between `% Iron Concentrate`, `% Silica Concentrate`, and `Ore Pulp pH`, capturing cyclical variations and chemical conditioning lags that monthly or daily aggregate dashboards completely mask.

### Step 3: Correlation & Feature Interdependence
Correlation matrix heatmaps revealed nonlinear relationships between chemical reagent dosage and impurity reduction, guiding future feature engineering for predictive quality control models.

### Step 4: Anomaly Detection & Process Stability
Identified transient spikes in reagent feed and pH shifts that correlate with degraded product purity, providing actionable levers for plant engineers to tighten process control limits.

---

## 💡 Engineering Recommendations & Business Impact

* **Dynamic Reagent Control:** High variance in Starch Flow suggests manual adjustments are lagging. Implementing automated closed-loop control based on real-time % Silica feedback can stabilize impurity rates.
* **Shift Handover Protocols:** Time-series shift analysis highlighted recurring instability during shift transitions on June 1, pointing toward an opportunity to standardize chemical conditioning protocols.
